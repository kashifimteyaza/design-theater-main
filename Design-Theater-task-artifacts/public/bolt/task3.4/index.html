The Process

From brief to delivery — every step managed

A structured lifecycle that protects both sides and keeps projects on track.

01

Post Your Brief

Write a structured project brief with budget, timeline, and deliverables. Our brief builder ensures you cover everything freelancers need to give you accurate proposals.

  • Guided brief templates by category
  • File attachment support
  • Budget range & timeline fields
02

Receive Proposals

Freelancers submit custom proposals — not just bids. Each includes their approach, relevant portfolio work, revised timeline, and a detailed cost breakdown.

  • Structured proposal format
  • Portfolio & past work samples
  • Compare proposals side-by-side
03

Interview & Select

Schedule video interviews directly on the platform. Use our structured interview guide to assess communication, expertise, and cultural fit before committing.

  • Built-in video call scheduling
  • Interview notes & scoring
  • Skills verification badges
04

Milestone Contracts

Auto-generate contracts with milestone-based payment schedules. Funds are held in escrow per milestone — released only when you approve the deliverable.

  • E-signed contracts & NDAs
  • Escrow per milestone
  • Scope change request flow
05

Pay & Retain

Release milestone payments with one click. Extend the engagement as a retainer, leave a detailed review, and build your trusted freelancer roster for future projects.

  • International wire & card payments
  • Auto-generated invoices
  • Retainer conversion in 2 clicks
