Meet your therapist

Dr. Sarah Chen, LMFT

I became a therapist because I believe that every family — no matter how fractured things feel right now — has the capacity for healing. My work is grounded in warmth, evidence-based methods, and a deep respect for each person's unique story.

Over 15 years, I've had the privilege of sitting with families in their most vulnerable moments and watching them find their way to something better. That never gets old for me.

Our philosophy

What therapy looks like here.

01

You're heard first.

Your first session is about listening. We won't jump to advice or diagnoses. We'll simply try to understand your world.

02

A plan built for you.

Together, we'll identify what change looks like for your family and design a therapeutic path that fits your pace and goals.

03

Real tools, real change.

Using EFT, Gottman methods, and CBT — we give you concrete skills to use between sessions, not just insight inside the room.

04

Progress you can feel.

We check in regularly on what's working. Therapy should feel meaningful — and you should see growth, not just talk about it.

Common questions

It's okay to have doubts.

The most common question we hear is: "Will this actually help?" Here are some honest answers.

If you're asking that question, it probably is. Therapy isn't reserved for crisis — it's for anyone who wants to improve their relationships and communication. Most people say they wish they'd started sooner.
Individual therapy is often a powerful starting point. Working on yourself genuinely changes relationship dynamics — and sometimes inspires others to join when they see the difference it makes.
That depends entirely on your goals and situation. Some families see significant change in 8–12 sessions. Others benefit from longer-term support. We discuss this openly from the start and regularly check in on your progress.
