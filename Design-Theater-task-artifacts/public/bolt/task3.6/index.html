Quality without gatekeeping

Great content rises naturally

We don't have a curriculum committee or a corporate approval process. Instead, the community itself surfaces the best content through transparent peer review, honest ratings, and a reputation system that rewards genuine helpfulness — not just seniority.

  • Peer reviewers vet every course for accuracy and clarity before it goes live
  • Ratings are verified: only enrolled students can rate
  • Wrong course? Wrong forum post? Easy correction paths — no data lost
  • Accessibility-first: transcripts, extended time options, and multiple formats
