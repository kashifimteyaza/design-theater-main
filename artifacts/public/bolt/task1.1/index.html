Our Story

A little shop with a lot of heart.

Three years ago we opened our doors on Maple Street with one idea: give this neighborhood a place that felt like home. Not a chain, not a concept — just great coffee, lovingly made, and space for people to think, connect, and slow down for a minute.

Every espresso is pulled to order. Every pastry arrives before dawn, made by hand in small batches. We know most of our regulars by name, and we can't wait to learn yours.
