Meet Dr. Sarah Chen

Licensed Family Therapist

With 15 years of experience in couples counseling, family mediation, and adolescent therapy, I've dedicated my career to helping families navigate their most challenging moments.

I believe that every relationship has the potential for growth and healing. My approach combines evidence-based techniques with genuine compassion, creating a therapeutic environment where real change can happen.
