Your Professional Image, Your Control

Learning shouldn't put your reputation at risk. Maybe you're a senior engineer learning design basics, or a manager exploring a career pivot. We get it.

Choose exactly who sees what you're learning, who you're learning from, and what progress you've made. Granular privacy controls mean you can explore freely without worrying about judgment.

Your LinkedIn connections won't see you're taking "Intro to Excel" unless you want them to.
