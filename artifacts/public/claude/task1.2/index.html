What We Offer

Find Your Flow

From mindful movement to strength building, we offer classes that challenge and nurture your body at every stage.

Yoga

⏱️ 60 min 👥 Max 12 📊 All Levels

Vinyasa flow, restorative, and power yoga classes designed to build strength, flexibility, and mental clarity. Perfect for beginners and seasoned yogis alike.

Pilates

⏱️ 50 min 👥 Max 10 📊 All Levels

Mat-based pilates focusing on core strength, posture, and controlled movement. Low-impact but incredibly effective for toning and stability.
