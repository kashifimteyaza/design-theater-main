The Challenge

Virtual events are hard. We make them human.

Every platform promises engagement. We've talked to hundreds of organizers and attendees to understand what actually breaks.

🕐

Time Zone Chaos

Attendees miss sessions because "2pm PST" means nothing in Tokyo. Our intelligent scheduling converts times automatically and sends smart reminders in each attendee's local timezone.

🚪

Wrong Room, Every Time

Getting lost between breakouts kills momentum. We provide persistent navigation, session breadcrumbs, and gentle redirects when someone wanders into the wrong virtual room.

😶

Awkward Networking

Forced video interactions feel unnatural. Our optional text-first networking, interest matching, and "low-pressure zones" let introverts connect comfortably while giving extroverts room to shine.

📉

Speaker Anxiety

Technical failures mid-presentation destroy confidence. We provide pre-session tech checks, automatic backup streams, and instant recovery from disconnects so speakers can focus on content.

🎯

Complexity Overload

Organizers drown in settings they don't understand. Our smart defaults work out of the box, with progressive disclosure—advanced features appear only when you're ready for them.

♿

Accessibility Afterthought

Screen readers, captions, and keyboard navigation shouldn't be add-ons. We build WCAG AA compliance into every feature from day one, not as a checkbox.

How We Solve It

The details that make it work

Every frustration we heard became a feature. Here's what makes EventSpace different.

Intelligent Session Recovery

Accidentally left a session? One-click rejoin takes you back to exactly where you were. Browser crashed? Auto-reconnect restores your session state, chat history, and Q&A position.

"I closed my laptop mid-session to move rooms. When I opened it, EventSpace had saved my spot and I didn't miss a thing." – Conference attendee

Multi-Speed Streaming

Attendees on slow connections get lower-quality video automatically—but see the same content in real-time. Presentation slides load separately from video so content is always readable.

Our adaptive streaming works on 3G connections without compromising the experience for those on fiber.

Context-Aware Navigation

The platform knows where you should be based on your agenda. Gentle prompts when sessions are starting, breadcrumb trails to prevent getting lost, and a "lost?" button that surfaces relevant sessions.

Navigation remembers your journey—back buttons actually work the way you expect.

Progressive Networking

Start with text chat, add video when comfortable, or stay text-only. See who shares your interests without awkward small talk. "Do Not Disturb" mode lets you watch without networking pressure.

Introverts appreciate the control. Extroverts appreciate the targeting. Everyone connects more meaningfully.

Smart Recording Permissions

Attendees control what's captured. Join with video off for networking but get prompted before Q&A if you'll be recorded. Post-event, download only the segments you participated in.

Privacy controls that respect consent without creating confusion.

Organizer Copilot

Live dashboard shows exactly what's happening: session health, attendee flow, engagement metrics. AI suggests interventions ("Session B has low attendance—send a reminder?") but never acts without permission.

Like having an experienced event producer watching your back 24/7.
